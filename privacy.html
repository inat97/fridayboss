Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No location, no contacts, no health data. Your workdays and cards stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • The times you clock in and out, and how many focus blocks you finish.
  • Your weekly runs: card picks, fight results, wins and rank.
  • Your settings: work hours, workdays, focus length, hero, reminders.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The widget and the focus Live Activity read it on the same device.

Notifications

Reminders (clock in, leave on time, focus done, fight open) are scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Friday Boss Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether a subscription is active.

Sharing

When you tap "Share the win", the app draws an image on your iPhone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice; nothing is sent to us.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like any other app, under Apple's privacy policy. We have no copy. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete — it is all on your device and under your control. Questions are still welcome: developers9x@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. If the app ever starts sending data off your device, this page will say so first and the app will ask you.

This page describes the app as built. It is not legal advice.
