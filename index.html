🃏Friday Boss

Every workweek is a card game.

A new boss arrives each Monday. Your real workday earns the cards: clock in, finish focus blocks, leave on time. On Friday after work, you fight the boss with the deck you built. Overtime only makes the boss stronger.

How it works

  • Clock in — one card pick (with an Uncommon if you start on time).
  • Focus blocks of 25 or 50 minutes — one pick each, up to four a day.
  • Leave on time — a pick with a guaranteed Rare. More than an hour late is overtime: no card, and the boss gets +10 HP.
  • Friday fight — a turn-based card battle when your last workday ends. Three tries, until Sunday night.
  • Career — every boss beaten moves you up from Intern to CEO and adds new cards.

Help

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel Pro. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Friday Boss Pro. Apple handles billing and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

I forgot to clock out. A shift left open is closed automatically at your end time a few hours later. It doesn't count as overtime and doesn't earn the on-time card.

Night shifts. Set an end time earlier than the start time (for example 22:00 → 06:00) and the shift ends the next morning.

Move or back up your progress. Everything lives only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup. There is no account or server to restore it from.
